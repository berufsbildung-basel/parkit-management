# Risks

This document contains project risks identified during the project.

Each risk is linked to the relevant workstream where the risk is being investigated or mitigated.

---

## LED Integration

**Risk**
- Facility Management may not approve the planned LED installation.
- The installation requires permission for mounting the LED panels, installing cables, connecting power, and making changes to the existing parking infrastructure.

**Impact**
- The LED integration may be delayed, require changes to the proposed installation, or may not be possible as currently planned.

**Mitigation**
- Prepare a detailed **project presentation** for Facility Management describing the planned solution, installation, cable routing, power supply, equipment locations, costs and responsibilities.
- Obtain approval before starting the installation.

**Related task**
- `Project Presentation & Administration Approval`

**Workstream**
- `workstreams/administration-approval/`

**Status** — In progress
