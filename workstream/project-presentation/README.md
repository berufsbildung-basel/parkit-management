## Project Presentation & Administration Approval

### Goal

Create a professional German-language project presentation for the landlord / Facility Management to present the planned parking solution and obtain approval for the installation.

---

### Scope

This work covers the preparation of the presentation and the supporting visual and technical information required for the approval process:
- project overview and intended use;
- mock-up of the planned solution;
- photos of the parking area with LED panels visualized;
- required components and installation work;
- project costs and confirmation that COMPANY covers all project-related costs;
- planned source of electricity;
- parking space layout and proposed cable routing;
- exact locations of LED panels, equipment and wiring.

---

### Requirements

- PDF format;
- official company branding;
- German language;
- realistic visual mock-ups and annotated plans

---

### Expected Outcome

A complete German-language presentation that:
- clearly explains the planned parking solution;
- shows the proposed installation and infrastructure;
- defines the required components, locations and installation work;
- clearly states the project's costs and responsibilities;
- can be submitted to the  Facility Management for approval.

---

### Working Materials

This directory contains the presentation, visual mock-ups, annotated plans, photos and other supporting materials prepared for the approval process.
