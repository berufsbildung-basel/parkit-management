## My Recommendation

### Option 1 – 2 × 64 × 64 P3

I recommend this option because:

1. We already have one panel.
    
2. The larger display is easier to read from a distance.
    

## Other Options

**Option 2:** Requires four panels, resulting in more wiring and a more complex setup.

**Option 3:** Smaller pixel pitch provides a sharper image, but the display is physically smaller.

**Option 4:** Good compromise between display size and sharpness, but requires two new panels.

## Conclusion

**Option 1 is the best choice** because we can reuse our existing panel and achieve a larger display with minimal additional hardware.
