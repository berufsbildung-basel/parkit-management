## Expiring Reservation Reminder

### Problem

Users may forget to leave their parking space when their reservation is about to expire.

---

### Goal

Implement an automated reminder system that checks active reservations and notifies users shortly before their reservation expires.

---

### Expected Behaviour

A background process periodically checks active reservations and sends a reminder approximately 10–15 minutes before expiration.

---

### Expected Outcome

Users receive a timely reminder before their reservation ends, helping reduce parking conflicts and unauthorized use of reserved spaces.

---

### Working Materials

This directory contains the research, design notes and other working materials related to the reservation reminder.
